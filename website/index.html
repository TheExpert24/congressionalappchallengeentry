About Us

Next Gen Opportunities is a database for academic opportunities. We have a list of over 200 opportunities + free online learning resources for ages K-University in over 22 different subjects. We created this site to help other parents or students look for different opportunities that would help cultivate their interests, since it is quite difficult to navigate through paid or scam opportunities for students.
